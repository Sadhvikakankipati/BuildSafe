ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

AI Techniques Used

👁️

Computer Vision

The safety module uses a CLIP vision-language model to compare construction images with safety-related descriptions.

Image → CLIP → Classification
🌳

Risk Prediction

The project-planning module evaluates duration, workers, budget, weather, material availability and complexity.

Project Data → Risk Model → Prediction

⚠️ Important Limitations

  • The visual model is a general-purpose vision-language model, not a specialized PPE detector.
  • Image quality, lighting and camera angle can affect AI results.
  • Project-risk prediction is a prototype and should not replace professional project management.
  • Real-world deployment would require domain-specific construction datasets and validation.
KEY TAKEAWAY

AI can help construction teams make faster, data-driven decisions.
